| 2026-10-21 23:30 | `2026-10-21_matrix-cap-32-and-lists-of-matrices.jpg` | Engine limits of the September 2026 matrices: Desmos refuses any matrix over 32 rows or 32 columns ("Matrices may not have more than 32 rows."). A list of matrices does work: `H = kQ for k=[1...1000]` holds 1000 matrices, `(HQ)[1000]` multiplies every one of them (batched matmul), `H[3][1;2]` double-indexes. Row gather with repeats, `Q[[2,1,2];]`, is a one-op embedding lookup. Also verified: a list of 1000 matrices of 32x32 (about 1M numbers in one expression) evaluates, far past the 10,000-element list cap. |

Weights are closed-form sines, so the attention is near uniform; a trained model will give real patterns. Reproduce: `experiments/attention-bench/`. |
